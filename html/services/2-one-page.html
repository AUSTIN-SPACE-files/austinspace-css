FAQ

Common questions

What if I need more than one page later?

The full £500 comes off a Custom Build if you upgrade within 18 months. Nothing gets thrown away either: moving from Basic to Core is a plan change on the same site, not a rebuild, so your domain, content and form all carry across and the page you started with becomes the homepage.

Do I actually own it?

Yes. The Squarespace subscription is in your name and paid by you from the day it goes live. If you ever want to take it elsewhere, or hand it to someone else, you can. Nothing is held hostage.

Who buys the domain?

You do, and it stays in your name. If you do not have one yet, the simplest route is taking it through Squarespace while the site is being set up, which means it is free for the first year and sits on the same account as everything else. One renewal date, one place to look. If you already own a domain, I will point it at the new site instead. Either way the wiring is my job and it is included.

What if I need something changed?

Email or text me. Small changes are £45, which covers up to half an hour and is most things. Anything bigger gets a price before I start. There is no monthly fee and no contract.

Can I have a blog?

Squarespace can turn one on, so yes if you want it. But a blog only helps if you actually post, and an abandoned one dated eighteen months ago does more harm than not having one at all. If you are serious about publishing regularly, that is usually the point where a Custom Build makes more sense.

Why Squarespace and not something cheaper?

Because it stays up and stays current without anyone having to maintain it. No security updates to chase, no plugins to break, nothing that stops working because a version number moved. You pay Squarespace directly at £12 a month, and with my partner discount your first year is £129.60 with the domain included.

How long does it really take?

One week from the point your form comes back. The form is the thing that sets the clock going, because the words come out of your answers.
